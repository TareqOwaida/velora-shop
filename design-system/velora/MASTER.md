# VELORA visual system

An editorial clothing store, expressed through generous whitespace, oversize type, fashion photography, and restrained motion.

- Canvas: warm ivory #f5f4ee.
- Ink: dark olive #22251f. Muted text: #66685f.
- Accent: muted champagne gold #c6ad83.
- Headings: Arial Black / Helvetica Neue; tight tracking and bold uppercase. Georgia italic adds an expressive secondary voice.
- Body: system Helvetica Neue / Arial for reliable local rendering.
- Shapes: mostly square product photography and controls, with round utility buttons and department filters.
- Homepage: split editorial hero, ticker, three departments, four featured pieces, champagne-gold manifesto, journal feature, and dark footer.
- Motion: GSAP entrance stagger, bounded hero parallax, viewport reveals, product image zooms, and short route transitions. Ticker has a pause control. All movement honors prefers-reduced-motion.
- Navigation: home, catalog, product, projects, saved pieces, bag, account and checkout; staff use a separate protected admin interface.
- Responsive layouts: desktop, tablet and mobile. Forms have labels, errors have alert roles, keyboard focus is visible, and main content has a skip link.
- Product photography is sample Unsplash content. Replace with real SKU images before launch.
